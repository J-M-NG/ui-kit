Chapter 4

Content style

How I write the words in the kit and in projects that use it. Keep it short and say what happens.

Voice

  • Write to the reader as “you”, in active sentences.
  • Headings name the topic; buttons name the outcome.
  • Put the useful part first, such as the number or what to do next.
  • Status tags are always a word. Colour is a second cue, not the only one.
  • No marketing words. Describe what the thing does.

Headings

Use sentence case. Someone skimming the headings alone should know what each section covers.

Do

Tasks due this week

Says what is listed and for when.

Avoid

Your tasks

Vague when the page already shows tasks.

Buttons and links

One primary button per view. Start with a verb and say what it acts on. Link text should say where the link goes, never “click here”.

Errors

Say what to fix, not what went wrong. Give an example when the format matters, and use the same words in the summary and beside the field.

Fix these fields

  • Enter a board name with at least 3 characters

Spelling and numbers

  • British spelling in the kit and its documentation (“colour”, “organise”). Product copy follows the product’s locale.
  • Numerals for all numbers in interfaces: “3 tasks”, not “three tasks”.
  • Dates as “24 November 2026”; times as “14:02”.
