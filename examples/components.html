On this page

A list of the page’s sections that stays in view while you scroll. On wide screens it’s a column beside the content, with its own scroll if the list is long. On phones it’s a bar at the top of the screen that opens to show the list. This page uses it.

The list works without JavaScript. With ui-kit.js, the section you are reading is marked in the list and named in the phone bar, and choosing a link closes the bar. If your project has a sticky header, set --jm-toc-offset to its height.

.jm-with-toc on <main> with __intro and __main; .jm-toc (a <nav> with aria-label) holding __details, __summary, __current, __title and __list. It also works inside .jm-app__sidebar, as on Composition.

Page layout

This page uses the layout classes: .jm-page (header, main and footer, footer at the bottom), .jm-skip-link, .jm-header, .jm-container (--wide), .jm-stack (--tight, --loose), .jm-cluster and .jm-footer. Press Tab once to see the skip link.
