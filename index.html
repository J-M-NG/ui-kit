Adopt the kit

  1. Copy ui-kit.css, and optionally ui-kit.js, into your project.
  2. Link it before your own stylesheet.
  3. Override the hooks, or load a file from presets/ after the kit.
  4. Check colour changes with tests/check_contrast.py --project your.css.
